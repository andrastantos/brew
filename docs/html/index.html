Todo

Fetch page size. The fetch unit ends bursts at 256-byte boundaries (page_bits = 7, counted in 16-bit words), but the external bus allows bursts within a 512-byte DRAM page. On the cycle model, 512 bytes halves the average cost of page ends (+0.30% to +0.16% against no page effects over the code-generation tests); 1024 bytes adds little. Confirm that every supported DRAM configuration has at least 512-byte pages, then change page_bits (RTL, cycle model, regenerated Verilog, rig suite).

(The original entry is located in todo.rst, line 51.)

Todo

Remaining Espresso cycle budget at -O2: ~43% refills after taken branches, ~16% fetch starvation, ~10% register waits. Ideas not yet evaluated: ending bursts early at fetched branches (the RTL’s break_on_branch_early option, currently off), cheaper restarts after a taken branch, and the cost of the 3-cycle result latency.

(The original entry is located in todo.rst, line 60.)

Todo

Branchless selects don’t pay on Espresso without a conditional move or set-on-compare (a branch over a move costs 3-5 cycles, a mask select 4-9 cycles and more on a full compare). Revisit if the ISA gains a select instruction, or once the scheduler can overlap independent select chains.

(The original entry is located in todo.rst, line 67.)

Todo
